This page manages content controls for the Apple TV that displayed this code. LoungeFeed filters some posts by default.

Preparing content controls

Checking the secure link for your Apple TV. If this message does not change, open the scanned link in Safari or another browser with JavaScript enabled.

Filtered posts

Some connected sources flag posts that may not be suitable for every viewer. Enabling this preference allows that Apple TV to offer an additional local screen-protection step.

Show filtered posts on Apple TVThe Apple TV keeps posts filtered until its local protection is completed.

A valid Pro membership is required for this preference to take effect. Without it, posts remain filtered.
