The Apple TV experience

Built around how you actually watch.

LoungeFeed is not a phone interface enlarged for television. Its navigation, information density, media layout, and focus behavior are designed around the remote and the room.

Browse

Move between sources without relearning the screen.

Twitter, Bluesky, Reddit, Mastodon, Telegram, and Pinterest share one television-first browsing language. Source and feed choices stay close at hand while the content remains central.

  • Large, readable post cards
  • Remote-first focus navigation
  • Remembered source and feed choices

Read and watch

Open the full post. Stay for the conversation.

Post details bring together the full text, engagement information, media, and replies. Portrait and landscape images keep their natural shape, while photos and videos can move to the full screen.

  • Expandable replies and long-form text
  • Galleries and full-screen media
  • Adaptive playback and quality controls

Favorites and collections

Keep the posts worth returning to.

Mark posts from supported sources as Favorites, or organize them into personal collections. LoungeFeed keeps a lightweight local snapshot for fast browsing and retrieves current details, replies, and full media when you open them.

Around the feed

Useful before and after you open the app.

Top Shelf can surface suitable recent posts on the Apple TV Home Screen. Explore mode teaches the major features before a Host is connected, and privacy-safe diagnostics make support easier when something goes wrong.

Your display

Make the screen fit the room.

Use the Host to choose the background, text size, spacing, media balance, and other viewing preferences. LoungeFeed automatically maintains readable contrast across light and dark display styles.

Local by design

From your accounts to Apple TV in three steps.

Install a Host

Run LoungeFeed Host on a Mac or iPhone connected to the same local network as Apple TV.

Connect your sources

Sign in through the source's own authorization experience and choose what you want to view.

Pair Apple TV

Select the discovered Host and enter its local six-digit pairing code.

Local-first privacy

Your Host keeps account access close to you.

Source sessions, tokens, feed cache, and media cache remain on the Host device. Apple TV receives a normalized, read-only viewing feed over the local network.
